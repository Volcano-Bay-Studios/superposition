Widgets are a collection of many small parts that can be placed on the surface of a [[Panel]]. They consist of Gauges, Buttons, Switches, Dials, and much more.

## Configuration 
Much like blocks, Widgets can be configured with a [[Screwdriver]] by (shift) right-clicking their tooltip. This allows you to change many options, including the name and color of every widget.
