Almost every block in Superposition interacts with Signals in one way or another.
Every signal has Frequency, Amplitude, and usually Data.

## Frequency
Many blocks in Superposition may create signals specifically to transfer data. These blocks with a standard frequency of **42 MHz**. A [[Signal Generator]] may be used to create a signal with a specific frequency.
