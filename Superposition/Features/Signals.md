Almost every block in Superposition interacts with Signals in one way or another.
Every signal has Frequency, Amplitude, and usually Data.

## Frequency
Many blocks in Superposition may create signals specifically to transfer data. These blocks with a standard frequency of **42 MHz**. ## Amplitude 
The Amplitude of a signal is important when broadcasting a signal as it has the biggest impact on the range of a signal. A signals amplitude is miniscule by default, and can be increased with an Amplifier. A signal that is not amplified will be too weak to be received by other antennas.
