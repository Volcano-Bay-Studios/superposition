# Panel
The Panel block allows placement of [[Widgets]] on the "panel" surface. 
## Configuration 
The Panel can have the front and back of its surface tilted by using a [[Screwdriver|Screwdriver]] to configure it. Additionally, you can place multiple panels side by side to connect them together.
